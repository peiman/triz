## Overview

Inventive Principle #2 (triz.org variant: "Extraction"): separate out an
interfering part/property, or keep only the necessary part.

## Sub-principles

- a) Separate an interfering part or property from an object, or single out the
  only necessary part (or property) of an object.

*(Tate & Domb letter this as a single sub-point; some lists split the
"interfering part" and "only necessary part" ideas into two.)*

## Examples

- Locate a noisy compressor outside the building where the compressed air is used.
- Use the sound of a barking dog (without the dog) as a burglar alarm.
