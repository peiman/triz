## Overview

In TRIZ, **resources** are any already-available substances, fields, space, time,
functions, or information — within the system, super-system, or environment —
that can be mobilized to perform a needed function at little or no cost. Because
the [[concept-ideality|Ideal Final Result]] demands maximum function with minimum
added cost, the ideal solution is built almost entirely from existing resources.
Resource analysis is the step that makes ideality actionable.

## Key Properties

- **Core categories:** substance, field/energy, space, time, functional,
  information. (Some authors add natural/environmental and system/super-system
  resources as separate groups — the six are the common core.)
- Resources may be **ready** (available), **derived** (transformable into a usable
  form), or **differential** (arising from differences between states/parameters).
- Voids/empty space and harmful or waste outputs count as resources ("turn harm
  into benefit"); [[concept-ariz|ARIZ]] treats empty space as a most-effective
  resource.
