## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "the
engineering system and its components evolve towards becoming more flexible,
dynamic, and adaptable — having more degrees of freedom." The evolutionary form
of [[principle-15-dynamics|Principle 15: Dynamics]].

## Key Properties

- Substance-dynamization progression (increasing degrees of freedom): monolith →
  spatial zones with different properties → one-joint → multi-joint → flexible/
  elastic → powder/liquid/gas → **field-based** (maximum dynamism / dematerialization).
- Maps to Altshuller's **Law of increasing dynamism** (direct descendant —
  inference).
