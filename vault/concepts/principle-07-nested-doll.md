## Overview

Inventive Principle #7 (triz.org variant: "Nesting"): place objects inside one
another, or pass one through a cavity in another.

## Sub-principles

- a) Place one object inside another; place each, in turn, inside the next.
- b) Make one part pass through a cavity in the other.

## Examples

- Russian nesting dolls; stacking measuring cups (a).
- Extending radio antenna; retractable aircraft landing gear stowed in the
  fuselage (b — also demonstrates [[principle-15-dynamics|Dynamics]]).
