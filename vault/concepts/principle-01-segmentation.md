## Overview

Inventive Principle #1 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: divide a system into independent or finer parts.

## Sub-principles

- a) Divide an object into independent parts.
- b) Make an object easy to disassemble.
- c) Increase the degree of fragmentation or segmentation.

## Examples

- Replace a mainframe with networked personal computers (a).
- Replace solid shades with Venetian blinds (c).

opposite direction). Resolves [[concept-technical-contradiction|technical
