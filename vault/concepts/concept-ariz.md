## Overview

**ARIZ** (Russian: АРИЗ) is the central, step-by-step analytical procedure of
classical TRIZ for solving **non-standard / hard inventive problems** — those a
direct lookup in the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]] or the
[[concept-76-standard-solutions|76 Standard Solutions]] cannot crack. It
transforms a vague situation into a precise problem model, then to an image of
the ideal solution, and finally to the analysis and resolution of the underlying
contradiction.

## Key Properties

- **Difference from the shortcut:** the matrix + [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Principles]]
  are a lookup for standardizable [[concept-technical-contradiction|technical
  contradictions]]; ARIZ is the full algorithm for problems where that fails,
  invoking the matrix/standards/effects merely as resources within its steps.
- **Canonical version: ARIZ-85C** — the last version published under Altshuller's
  name and the one MATRIZ treats as official.
- **Structure (ARIZ-85C, 9 parts):** (1) analyze the problem → mini-problem &
  technical contradictions; (2) analyze the problem model → operating zone (OZ),
  operating time (OT), substance-field resources (SFR); (3) formulate the
  [[concept-ideality|Ideal Final Result]] and the
  [[concept-physical-contradiction|physical contradiction]]; (4) mobilize
  substance-field [[concept-resources|resources]]; (5) apply the knowledge base
  (standards, [[concept-separation-principles|separation principles]], effects);
  (6) change or substitute the problem; (7) analyze the resolution method;
  (8) capitalize on the solution concept; (9) analyze the problem-solving process
  (feed learnings back). Often grouped as restructure (1–3) → remove (4–6) →
  analyze (7–9).
- **Evolution:** developed through ~a dozen versions from ARIZ-56 to ARIZ-85C
  (1956–1985). An "ARIZ-85D" is **unverified** (the A–D sub-modifications belong
  to ARIZ-82).

## Connections

The algorithmic spine that ties together contradictions, IFR/ideality,
su-field analysis, the standards, separation principles, and resources.
