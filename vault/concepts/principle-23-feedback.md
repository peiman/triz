## Overview

Inventive Principle #23 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: introduce or change feedback to regulate a process.

## Sub-principles

- a) Introduce feedback (referring back, cross-checking) to improve a process or
  action.
- b) If feedback is already used, change its magnitude or influence.

## Examples

- Automatic volume control in audio circuits (a).
- A thermostat / statistical process control adjusting based on measurement (a).
