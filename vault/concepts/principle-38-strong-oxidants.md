## Overview

Inventive Principle #38 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: escalate oxidation along a ladder from ambient air to ozone.

## Sub-principles

A graded escalation:
- a) Replace common air with oxygen-enriched air.
- b) Replace enriched air with pure oxygen.
- c) Expose air or oxygen to ionizing radiation.
- d) Use ionized oxygen.
- e) Replace ozonized/ionized oxygen with ozone.

*(Sources disagree on whether the ladder is lettered A–E or A–F and on inclusion
of "singlet oxygen" — the concept ladder is reliable; exact lettering is flagged.)*

## Examples

- Use oxygen (not air) with an acetylene torch for a hotter, cleaner cut.
- Ozone treatment of water for disinfection.

## Connections

Conceptual opposite-pair with [[principle-39-inert-atmosphere|Inert Atmosphere]]
(accelerate vs. suppress interaction).
