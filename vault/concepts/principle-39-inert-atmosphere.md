## Overview

Inventive Principle #39 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: replace the active environment with an inert one to suppress
unwanted interaction.

## Sub-principles

- a) Replace a normal environment with an inert one.
- b) Add neutral parts or inert additives to an object.
- c) Carry out the process in a vacuum.

## Examples

- Fill an incandescent bulb with argon to prevent filament oxidation (a).
- Store/handle reactive materials under a nitrogen blanket (a/b).

## Connections

Conceptual opposite-pair with [[principle-38-strong-oxidants|Strong Oxidants]].
