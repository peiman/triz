## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "as an
engineering system evolves, flow rates of substances, energy, or information
increase, and/or the flows are better utilized."

## Key Properties

- Two sub-trends: **improving useful flows** (increase conductivity; make better
  use of the flow) and **reducing harmful/accidental flows** (decrease their
  conductivity; reduce their impact).
- One of the four mechanisms MATRIZ names as directly supporting the
  [[trend-increasing-value|increasing-value]] trend.
- Maps to Altshuller's **Law of energy conductivity**, broadened to
  substance/energy/information flows (descendant — inference).

## Connections

Supports [[trend-increasing-value|increasing value]]; the broadened
energy-conductivity law.
