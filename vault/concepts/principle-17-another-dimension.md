## Overview

Inventive Principle #17 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: move into extra dimensions, multi-story layouts, or another side.

## Sub-principles

- a) Move an object in two- or three-dimensional space.
- b) Use a multi-story arrangement instead of a single-story one.
- c) Tilt or re-orient the object; lay it on its side.
- d) Use "another side" of a given area.

## Examples

- A five-axis cutting tool positioned where needed (a).
- A dump truck tilting its bed (c); stacking microelectronic circuits to improve
  density (d).
