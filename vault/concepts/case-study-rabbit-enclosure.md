## Overview

A TRIZ **teaching example** of resolving a
[[concept-physical-contradiction|physical contradiction]] by
[[concept-separation-principles|separation in space]].

## Problem

Rabbits need continuous access to fresh grass but must be contained; a farmer
cannot continuously deliver fresh fodder to a fixed cage.

## TRIZ framing

- **Physical contradiction:** the enclosure must be *fixed* (to contain the
  rabbits) and *not fixed* (so they reach fresh, ungrazed grass).
- **Ideal Final Result:** "the rabbits feed themselves fresh grass" — the system
  delivers fodder by itself, no farmer action.

## Solution & tools

- **Separation in space** (the [[concept-separation-principles|separation
  principles]] resolve the physical contradiction): put the enclosure **on
  wheels** so it relocates to fresh grazing — a mobile grazing unit. Also readable
  as [[principle-15-dynamics|Principle 15: Dynamics]] (static → movable).
- Quantified outcome: **not documented** (teaching illustration).
