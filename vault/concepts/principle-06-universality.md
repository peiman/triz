## Overview

Inventive Principle #6 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: make a part perform multiple functions, eliminating other parts.

## Sub-principles

- a) Make a part or object perform multiple functions; eliminate the need for
  other parts.

## Examples

- The handle of a toothbrush contains toothpaste.
- A child's car safety seat converts to a stroller.

## Connections

Frequently paired with [[principle-05-merging|Merging]] (e.g. a mulching
lawnmower demonstrates both).
