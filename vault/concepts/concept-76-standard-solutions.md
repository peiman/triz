## Overview

The **76 Standard Solutions** (Standard Inventive Solutions / System of
Standards) are a classified set of typical, generalized solution-models matched
to typical problem-models expressed as [[concept-su-field-analysis|su-field]]
models. Once a problem is cast as a su-field of a given state, the analyst looks
up the standard(s) that resolve that state — a directed, repeatable path to a
solution concept. Compiled by Altshuller and associates between **1975 and 1985**.

## Key Properties

- **Five classes** (MATRIZ names; per-class counts sum to 76):
  1. Building and destruction of Su-Fields — *13*
  2. Development of Su-Fields — *23*
  3. Transition to super-system and micro-level — *6*
  4. Standards for measuring and detecting — *17*
  5. Standards on application of standards (helpers toward
     [[concept-ideality|ideality]]) — *17*
- **"76" is the canonical count**, but detailed enumeration with sub-items runs
  to ~86 entries; modern reformulations (e.g. a proposed 111-standard set) are
  **not** part of Altshuller's canonical 76.
- Inseparable from [[concept-su-field-analysis|su-field analysis]] (the language);
  typically applied as a step within [[concept-ariz|ARIZ]] for standard problems
  (the ARIZ-integration detail is single-strong-sourced — medium-high confidence).
