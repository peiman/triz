## Overview

**Su-Field (Substance-Field) Analysis** is the modeling language of TRIZ. A
minimal function or problem is represented as a triangle of three elements: a
**substance S1** (the object acted upon), a **substance S2** (the tool), and a
**field F** (the energy enabling their interaction — mechanical, thermal,
chemical, electrical, magnetic, acoustic, optical, etc.). The premise: a workable
function needs two substances plus a field.

The Russian original term is **Vepol** (ВЕПОЛЬ, *veshchestvo* "substance" +
*pole* "field"); "Su-Field" / "S-Field" are the standard English renderings.

## Key Properties

- **Model states:** *complete & effective* (goal); *incomplete* (an element is
  missing → add it); *harmful* (interaction produces an unwanted effect →
  introduce a third substance, modify one, or add a counteracting field);
  *insufficient/ineffective* (useful effect too weak → upgrade/substitute the
  field or substance, or move to a chain/double su-field).
- It is TRIZ's bridge from a messy real problem to a **typed problem model**: the
  model state selects which class of the
  [[concept-76-standard-solutions|76 Standard Solutions]] applies.
- Used within [[concept-ariz|ARIZ]]'s analysis phase; substance and field
  [[concept-resources|resources]] are its building blocks; the field is often
  chosen via the [[concept-effects-database|effects database]].

## Connections

The language; the [[concept-76-standard-solutions|76 Standard Solutions]] are the
