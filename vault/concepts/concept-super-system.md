## Overview

In TRIZ, a technical system is always part of a larger **super-system** (its
environment and neighbouring systems) and is itself composed of **sub-systems**.
This three-level view — sub-system → system → super-system — is a core analytical
frame: problems and [[concept-resources|resources]] are sought across all three
levels.

## Key Properties

- **Transition to the super-system** is one of the
  [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|laws of system evolution]]: when
  a system exhausts its own potential, it integrates into a super-system
  (mono → bi → poly-system).
- Super-system resources (adjacent substances, fields, functions) are prime
  candidates for ideal, low-cost solutions.
- Reformulating a problem at the super-system level is an explicit move in
  [[concept-ariz|ARIZ]] (Part 6).

## Connections

The upward direction of system structure; tied to the evolution laws and
