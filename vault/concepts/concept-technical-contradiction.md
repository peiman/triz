## Overview

A **technical (engineering) contradiction** occurs when an attempt to improve one
parameter of a system causes another parameter to deteriorate — e.g. increasing
strength increases weight. It is a conflict *between two different parameters*.
Identifying the contradiction at the heart of a problem is TRIZ's founding move
([[source-altshuller-shapiro-1956|Altshuller & Shapiro 1956]]).

## Key Properties

- Conflict **between two parameters**: improving A worsens B.
- Resolved using the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]]: map A
  and B onto the [[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]],
  then apply the suggested [[concept-40-inventive-principles|Inventive Principles]].
- A defining TRIZ goal is to resolve the contradiction **without compromise** —
  contrasted with conventional engineering optimization, which accepts trade-offs.
- A technical contradiction often has a deeper
  [[concept-physical-contradiction|physical contradiction]] as its root cause.
