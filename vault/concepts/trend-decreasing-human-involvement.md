## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "as an
engineering system evolves, the number of engineering system functions performed
by humans decreases." MATRIZ classifies it as a **sub-trend of
[[trend-increasing-completeness|increasing completeness]]**.

## Key Properties

- Functions are taken over from the human in sequence: **transmission → energy
  source → control system → decision-making**.
- As the system increases its completeness it takes over these functions, thereby
  reducing human involvement.
- A **new emphasis** (automation/autonomy) with no clean one-to-one Altshuller-law
  ancestor — flagged.

## Connections
