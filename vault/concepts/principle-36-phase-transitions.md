## Overview

Inventive Principle #36 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: exploit the phenomena occurring *during* a phase change (volume
change, latent heat).

## Sub-principles

- a) Use phenomena occurring during phase transitions (volume changes, release or
  absorption of heat, etc.).

## Examples

- Use water-to-steam expansion (or freezing expansion) to do work; a heat pump
  exploiting latent heat.
- Expansion of freezing water to split rock (illustrative).

## Connections

A refinement of [[principle-35-parameter-changes|Parameter Changes]] — it uses
the *transition itself*, not just the end state.
