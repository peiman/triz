## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "as an
engineering system evolves, development is concentrated on the operating agent
first, and on the rest of the system later." A sub-trend of
[[trend-increasing-coordination|increasing coordination]].

## Key Properties

- The engine of evolution (MATRIZ, verbatim): "The evolution of a system is driven
  by resolving [[concept-technical-contradiction|contradictions]], while
  contradictions themselves arise from the uneven development of the system's
  components." The mismatch triggers further development, and the cycle continues.
- Maps to Altshuller's **Law of uneven development of parts** (direct descendant —
  inference).
