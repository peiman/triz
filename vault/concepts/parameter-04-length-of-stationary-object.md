## Overview

Engineering Parameter #4 of the [[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]] — any one linear dimension of a stationary object. The 39 parameters form both axes of the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]]; a real problem is translated into them to look up [[concept-40-inventive-principles|inventive principles]].

## Connections

The moving/stationary counterpart of [[parameter-03-length-of-moving-object|Length of Moving Object]] within the 39 parameters.
