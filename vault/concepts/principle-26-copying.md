## Overview

Inventive Principle #26 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: use a cheap or optical copy instead of the real, expensive, or
fragile object.

## Sub-principles

- a) Instead of an unavailable, expensive, or fragile object, use simpler,
  inexpensive copies.
- b) Replace an object or process with optical copies (which can be scaled).
- c) If visible optical copies are used, move to infrared or ultraviolet copies.

*(The exact a/b/c split varies between the triz.org and Tate & Domb redactions
— flagged.)*

## Examples

- Measure a tall object by measuring its photograph or shadow.
- Virtual prototypes / simulations in place of physical ones (illustrative).
