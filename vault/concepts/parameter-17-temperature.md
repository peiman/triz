## Overview

Engineering Parameter #17 of the [[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]] — the thermal condition of an object or system. The 39 parameters form both axes of the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]]; a real problem is translated into them to look up [[concept-40-inventive-principles|inventive principles]].
