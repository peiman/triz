## Overview

Inventive Principle #19 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: use periodic or pulsed action instead of continuous.

## Sub-principles

- a) Instead of continuous action, use periodic or pulsating actions.
- b) If an action is already periodic, change its magnitude or frequency.
- c) Use the pauses between impulses to perform a different action.

## Examples

- Replace a continuous siren with a pulsed sound (a).
- In CPR, breathe after every few chest compressions (c).

## Connections

Contrast with [[principle-20-continuity-of-useful-action|Continuity of Useful
Action]].
