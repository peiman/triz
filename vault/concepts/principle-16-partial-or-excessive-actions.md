## Overview

Inventive Principle #16 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: if exactly 100% is hard, do slightly less or slightly more.

## Sub-principles

- a) If 100% of an effect is hard to achieve, using "slightly less" or "slightly
  more" of the same method may make the problem much easier.

## Examples

- Over-spray when painting, then remove the excess (or use a stencil).
- Fill, then "top off," when filling a gas tank.
