## Overview

Engineering Parameter #3 of the [[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]] — any one linear dimension of a movable object. The 39 parameters form both axes of the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]]; a real problem is translated into them to look up [[concept-40-inventive-principles|inventive principles]].

## Connections

The moving/stationary counterpart of [[parameter-04-length-of-stationary-object|Length of Stationary Object]] within the 39 parameters.
