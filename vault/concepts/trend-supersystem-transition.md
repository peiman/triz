## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "as an
engineering system evolves, it is integrated with supersystem components." See
[[concept-super-system|Super-system]].

## Key Properties

- Sub-mechanisms: increasing differentiation of parameters; differentiation
  between main functions (allied → heterogeneous → inverse); deeper integration
  (unlinked → partially trimmed → completely trimmed); and increasing the number
  of integrated systems — the **mono-system → bi-system → poly-system** progression.
- Maps to Altshuller's **Law of transition to the super-system** (direct
  descendant — inference).

## Connections

The upward-integration trend; tied to [[concept-super-system|super-system]] and
[[concept-trimming|trimming]].
