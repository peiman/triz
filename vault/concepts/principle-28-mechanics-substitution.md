## Overview

Inventive Principle #28 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: replace a mechanical means with a sensory or field-based one —
closely tied to [[concept-su-field-analysis|su-field]] thinking.

## Sub-principles

- a) Replace a mechanical means with a sensory (optical, acoustic, thermal,
  olfactory) means.
- b) Use electric, magnetic, and electromagnetic fields to interact with the
  object.
- c) Change from static to movable fields, from unstructured to structured ones.
- d) Use fields together with field-activated (e.g. ferromagnetic) particles.

*(Sub-principles c/d are the Tate & Domb four-part form; the triz.org redaction
surfaces only a/b — flagged.)*

## Examples

- Add an odorant to odorless natural gas so leaks are detected by smell (a).
- A magnetic stirrer driving fluid via an electromagnetic field (b).
