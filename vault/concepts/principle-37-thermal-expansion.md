## Overview

Inventive Principle #37 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: use thermal expansion/contraction, possibly with multiple materials.

## Sub-principles

- a) Use thermal expansion (or contraction) of materials.
- b) If using thermal expansion, use multiple materials with different expansion
  coefficients.

## Examples

- A bimetallic strip in a thermostat converts temperature into movement (b).
- Heat a ring to expand it, fit it, then let it shrink onto a shaft (a).
