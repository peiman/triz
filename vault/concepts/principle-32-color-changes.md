## Overview

Inventive Principle #32 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: change the color or transparency of an object or its environment.

## Sub-principles

- a) Change the color of an object or its environment.
- b) Change the transparency of an object or its environment.
- c) Use colored additives to observe something hard to see.
- d) If such additives are used, employ luminescent traces or tracer atoms.

## Examples

- A transparent bandage lets a wound be inspected without removal (b).
- A red safelight in a photographic darkroom (a).
