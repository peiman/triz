## Overview

Inventive Principle #35 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: change a physical state, concentration, flexibility, or
temperature. The single **most frequently recommended** principle in the
[[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]].

## Sub-principles

- a) Change an object's physical state (e.g. to gas, liquid, solid).
- b) Change the concentration or consistency.
- c) Change the degree of flexibility.
- d) Change the temperature.

*(A fifth "change the degree of automation" item appears in some redactions but
is **not** part of the canonical Tate & Domb #35.)*

## Examples

- Freeze the liquid centers of filled candies, then dip in chocolate, instead of
  handling hot liquid (a).
- Raise temperature past the Curie point to make a ferromagnet paramagnetic (d).
