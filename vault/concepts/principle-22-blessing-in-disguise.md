## Overview

Inventive Principle #22 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: turn a harmful factor into a useful one — a core
[[concept-resources|resource]] move.

## Sub-principles

- a) Use harmful factors (especially of the environment) to achieve a positive
  effect.
- b) Eliminate a harmful action by combining it with another harmful action.
- c) Amplify a harmful factor until it is no longer harmful.

## Examples

- Use waste heat to generate power (a).
- Backfire — use fire to remove fuel ahead of a forest fire (a).

## Connections

Embodies the "turn harm into benefit" idea central to
[[concept-resources|resource]] analysis.
