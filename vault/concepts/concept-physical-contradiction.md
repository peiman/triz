## Overview

A **physical contradiction** occurs when one and the same parameter of a system
is required to take two opposite, mutually exclusive values at once — e.g. a
coffee-cup wall must be *thick* to insulate the hand and *thin* to be light. It
is a conflict *within a single parameter*, and in TRIZ is the deeper root cause
underlying a [[concept-technical-contradiction|technical contradiction]].

## Key Properties

- Conflict **within one parameter**: it must be both X and not-X.
- Resolved using the **Separation Principles**: separation in time, in space,
  upon condition, and between system levels (parts vs. whole).
- Reaching the controlling physical contradiction beneath a surface technical
  contradiction is a central aim of **ARIZ** (Algorithm of Inventive Problem
  Solving, notably ARIZ-85).

## Connections

The fundamental counterpart to [[concept-technical-contradiction|Technical
Contradiction]]; resolving it advances the system toward
[[concept-ideality|ideality]].
