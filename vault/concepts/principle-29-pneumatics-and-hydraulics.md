## Overview

Inventive Principle #29 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: replace solid parts with gas or liquid.

## Sub-principles

- a) Use gas and liquid parts instead of solid ones (inflatable, fluid-filled,
  air cushion, hydrostatic, hydro-reactive).

## Examples

- Gel-filled or air-cushion shoe insoles.
- Air suspension on vehicles (illustrative).

## Connections

Frequently co-occurs with [[principle-30-flexible-shells-and-thin-films|Flexible
Shells and Thin Films]] (both substitute compliant media for rigid structure).
