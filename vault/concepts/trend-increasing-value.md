## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "as an
engineering system evolves its value always increases." The **apex** of the
MATRIZ TESE hierarchy — all other trends are supporting mechanisms beneath it.

## Key Properties

- Value formula: **Value = Σ Functionality / Σ Cost** (directly proportional to
  functionality, inversely to cost) — the operational form of
  [[concept-ideality|ideality]].
- Stage-specific strategies: S1 raise functionality + cut cost; S2 functionality
  grows faster than cost; S3 cut cost at stable functionality; S4 reduce
  functionality to enable large cost cuts.
- Maps to Altshuller's **Law of increasing ideality** (direct descendant —
  inference).

## Connections
