## Overview

**Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD)** is a demand-side discovery framework — customers "hire" a
product to make progress on a *job*. TRIZ is a supply-side *invention* framework —
how to build inventively once you know the problem. They are complementary: JTBD
finds the *right* problem; TRIZ resolves it. JTBD bridges the gap TRIZ tools assume
away (they presume you already know the problem).

## Key Properties

- **Structural parallel:** a JTBD *job* ≈ a TRIZ **main useful function**; JTBD
  *desired outcomes* ≈ TRIZ parameters/criteria to improve; **over-served vs
  under-served outcomes** ≈ a TRIZ **contradiction** (improving an under-served
  outcome worsens an over-served one).
- **ODI job maps** (Ulwick) decompose a job into measurable outcomes, which seed the
  function hierarchy for [[concept-function-analysis|function analysis]] and reveal
  where [[concept-ideality|ideality]] is low.
- **Front-end of the workflow:** discovering unmet/under-served jobs supplies the
  "what to solve" that the
  [[concept-triz-product-development-workflow|TRIZ workflow]]'s analytical tools then
  attack.
- **Honest boundary:** JTBD says *what to build*; TRIZ says *how to build it
  inventively*. Neither replaces the other.

## Connections

The demand-side bridge into the
[[concept-triz-product-development-workflow|product-development workflow]]; turns a
market need into a function/contradiction TRIZ can act on.
