## Overview

Inventive Principle #12 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: in a potential field, avoid the need to change an object's position.

## Sub-principles

- a) In a potential field, limit position changes (e.g. change operating
  conditions to eliminate the need to raise or lower objects in gravity).

## Examples

- Canal locks move boats between water levels (Panama Canal).
- "Skillets" in an auto plant bring tools to the right working height (also
  demonstrates [[principle-10-preliminary-action|Preliminary Action]]).
