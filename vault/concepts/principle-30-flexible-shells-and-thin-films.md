## Overview

Inventive Principle #30 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: use flexible shells and thin films instead of 3-D structures, and
to isolate from the environment.

## Sub-principles

- a) Use flexible shells and thin films instead of three-dimensional structures.
- b) Isolate the object from its external environment using flexible shells and
  thin films.

## Examples

- A thin-film greenhouse cover instead of a rigid structure (a).
- An inflatable cover over a tennis court (a).
