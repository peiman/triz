## Overview

Inventive Principle #24 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: use an intermediary carrier or process, possibly removed later.

## Sub-principles

- a) Use an intermediary carrier article or intermediary process.
- b) Merge one object temporarily with another (which can be easily removed).

## Examples

- A carpenter's nailset between hammer and nail (a).
- A pot holder to carry hot dishes (a).
