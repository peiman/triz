## Overview

A walkthrough of the nine parts of **[[concept-ariz|ARIZ]]-85C** — the canonical
algorithm for non-standard inventive problems. Part titles are verbatim from the
MATRIZ-published [[source-altshuller-ariz-85c|ARIZ-85C text]]. The three blocks
are: **restructure the problem** (Parts 1–3), **remove the contradiction**
(Parts 4–6), and **analyze the solution** (Parts 7–9).

## Part 1 — Analyzing the problem

Turn a vague situation into a precise *Problem Model*: formulate the
**mini-problem** (everything stays unchanged or simpler, yet the required action
appears / the harmful one disappears); state the two
[[concept-technical-contradiction|technical contradictions]] TC-1 and TC-2;
choose a graphical conflict model; introduce the **X-element**; and try the
[[concept-76-standard-solutions|System of Standard Inventive Solutions]] first.

## Part 2 — Analyzing the problem model

Inventory what is available: define the **Operating Zone (OZ)**, the **Operating
Time (OT)**, and the **Substance-Field [[concept-resources|Resources]] (SFR)**.

## Part 3 — Formulating the IFR and physical contradiction

State **IFR-1** and enforce it; formulate the
[[concept-physical-contradiction|physical contradiction]] at the **macro** and
**micro** levels; then state **IFR-2**, crystallizing the now-physical problem.

## Part 4 — Mobilizing Substance-Field Resources

Use [[concept-smart-little-people|Smart Little People]] modeling, "step back"
from the IFR, and exploit substance mixtures, **empty space/void** (the most
effective resource), derived resources, and fields.

## Part 5 — Applying the knowledge base

Apply the [[concept-76-standard-solutions|76 Standard Solutions]], analogous
solved problems, the [[concept-separation-principles|Separation Principles]], and
the library of [[concept-effects-database|scientific effects]].

## Part 6 — Changing or substituting the problem

Convert a theoretical to a practical solution, untangle problem clusters, switch
to the other contradiction, or reformulate the mini-problem at the
[[concept-super-system|super-system]] level.

## Part 7 — Analyzing the resolution method

Quality-check the solution concept against IFR-1, novelty (patent search), and
any secondary problems introduced.

## Part 8 — Capitalizing on the solution concept

Generalize the solution; apply it to the super-system and to other problems;
build a morphological matrix of uses.

## Part 9 — Analyzing the problem-solving process

Compare the actual path taken against the theoretical ARIZ flow, and feed any
new principles back into the TRIZ knowledge base (the
[[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|feedback-cycle]] discipline).

## Connections

The detailed procedure behind [[concept-ariz|ARIZ]]; drives a problem down to the
[[concept-physical-contradiction|physical contradiction]] where the
[[concept-separation-principles|separation principles]] resolve it.
