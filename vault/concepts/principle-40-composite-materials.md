## Overview

Inventive Principle #40 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: change from uniform materials to composite (multiple) ones.

## Sub-principles

- a) Change from uniform (homogeneous) to composite (multiple) materials.

## Examples

- Carbon-fiber composite aircraft components for a high strength-to-weight ratio.
- Fiberglass; composite armor (illustrative).

## Connections

Conceptual opposite-pair with [[principle-33-homogeneity|Homogeneity]]
(mixed-material vs. same-material).
