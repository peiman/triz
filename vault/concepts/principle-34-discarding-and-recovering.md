## Overview

Inventive Principle #34 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: discard parts once their function is done, or restore consumable
parts during operation.

## Sub-principles

- a) Make portions that have served their purpose go away (dissolve, evaporate)
  or modify them directly during operation.
- b) Conversely, restore consumable parts directly during operation.

## Examples

- Rocket booster stages jettisoned after use; a dissolvable medicine capsule (a).
- A self-sharpening blade that restores its edge during use (b).
