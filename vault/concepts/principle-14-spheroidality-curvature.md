## Overview

Inventive Principle #14 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: replace straight lines/flat surfaces with curves, spheres, and
rotary motion.

## Sub-principles

- a) Use curvilinear parts instead of rectilinear ones; move from flat to
  spherical surfaces, from cubes to balls.
- b) Use rollers, balls, spirals, domes.
- c) Go from linear to rotary motion; use centrifugal force.

## Examples

- Arches and domes for strength in architecture (a).
- Spin clothes to remove water instead of wringing (c).
