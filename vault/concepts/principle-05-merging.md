## Overview

Inventive Principle #5 (triz.org variant: "Consolidation"): bring identical or
similar objects/operations together in space or time.

## Sub-principles

- a) Bring closer together (or merge) identical or similar objects; assemble
  identical or similar parts to perform parallel operations.
- b) Make operations contiguous or parallel; bring them together in time.

## Examples

- Thousands of microprocessors in a parallel-processor computer (a).
- Link slats together in Venetian or vertical blinds (b).

## Connections

The opposite direction of [[principle-01-segmentation|Segmentation]]; frequently
paired with [[principle-06-universality|Universality]].
