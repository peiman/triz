## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "as an
engineering system evolves, system elements (components or operations) are
eliminated without impairing the functionality of the system, and possibly
improving it." The evolutionary form of [[concept-trimming|Trimming]].

## Key Properties

- Mechanisms: trim function blocks (transmission, energy source, control,
  operating agent — in that order); trim operations (corrective → providing →
  productive functions); trim lowest-value components first.
- Becomes more relevant in later S-curve stages (maturity/decline).
- A **new emphasis** operationalizing the ideality/value drive; no direct
  classic-8 ancestor — flagged.

## Connections

The evolutionary trend behind the [[concept-trimming|Trimming]] tool.
