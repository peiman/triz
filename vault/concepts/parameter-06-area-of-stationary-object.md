## Overview

Engineering Parameter #6 of the [[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]] — the surface or area associated with a stationary object. The 39 parameters form both axes of the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]]; a real problem is translated into them to look up [[concept-40-inventive-principles|inventive principles]].

## Connections

The moving/stationary counterpart of [[parameter-05-area-of-moving-object|Area of Moving Object]] within the 39 parameters.
