## Overview

Inventive Principle #11 (triz.org variant: "Cushion in Advance"): prepare
emergency means in advance to offset a system's low reliability.

## Sub-principles

- a) Prepare emergency means beforehand to compensate for the relatively low
  reliability of an object.

## Examples

- A back-up parachute.
- A magnetic strip on photographic film directing the developer to compensate
  for poor exposure (illustrative).
