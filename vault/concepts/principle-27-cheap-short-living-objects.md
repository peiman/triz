## Overview

Inventive Principle #27 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: replace one expensive object with many cheap ones, trading off some
property (e.g. durability).

## Sub-principles

- a) Replace an expensive object with a multiple of inexpensive objects,
  compromising other properties (such as service life).

## Examples

- Disposable paper cups; disposable diapers.
- Single-use razors / surgical masks (illustrative).
