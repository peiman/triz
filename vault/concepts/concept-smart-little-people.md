## Overview

**Smart Little People (SLP)** is a classical Altshuller modeling technique: the
solver imagines the operating zone as a crowd of tiny intelligent creatures that
can be commanded to behave however the solution requires. By having the "little
people" divide, hold, separate, or move, the solver visualizes a new arrangement
that resolves the problem — escaping [[concept-psychological-inertia|psychological
inertia]].

## Key Properties

- Replace the conflict zone with a swarm of small intelligent agents; let them
  act out the ideal behaviour, then translate the picture back into a technical
  solution.
- Designed to fix the **"empathy trap"** of Gordon's Synectics "become the
  object" method: imagining you *are* the machine triggers a self-preservation
  bias against destructive actions. The little people can be divided/destroyed
  freely, so ideas of division and segmentation come easily.
- Best applied at the operating zone/time, often after stating the
  [[concept-physical-contradiction|physical contradiction]] or the
  [[concept-ideality|IFR]].
- Classical (Altshuller); sibling to the [[concept-system-operator|System
  Operator]] in the psychological-inertia family.
