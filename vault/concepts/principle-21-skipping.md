## Overview

Inventive Principle #21 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: run a harmful or hazardous stage at high speed to skip its damage.

## Sub-principles

- a) Conduct a process, or certain stages (especially destructive, harmful, or
  hazardous ones), at high speed.

## Examples

- A high-speed dental drill avoids overheating tissue.
- Cut plastic faster than heat can propagate, so it does not deform or melt.
