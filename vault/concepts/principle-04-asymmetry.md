## Overview

Inventive Principle #4 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: break or increase asymmetry in an object's shape.

## Sub-principles

- a) Change the shape of an object from symmetrical to asymmetrical.
- b) If an object is already asymmetrical, increase its degree of asymmetry.

## Examples

- Asymmetrical mixing vessels / asymmetrical vanes improve mixing (cement trucks,
  blenders) (a).
- Change circular O-rings to oval or specialized cross-sections to improve
  sealing (b).

## Connections

Often co-occurs with [[principle-08-anti-weight|Anti-weight]] (e.g. asymmetric
wing shape generating lift).
