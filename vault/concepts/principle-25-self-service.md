## Overview

Inventive Principle #25 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: make an object serve itself and use its own waste — a route toward
[[concept-ideality|ideality]].

## Sub-principles

- a) Make an object serve itself by performing auxiliary helpful functions.
- b) Use waste resources, energy, or substances.

## Examples

- A halogen lamp regenerates its filament during use (a).
- Animal waste used as fertilizer; using process waste heat (b).

## Connections

Strongly tied to [[concept-resources|resources]] and
[[concept-ideality|ideality]] (the ideal system serves itself).
