## Overview

A peer-reviewed industrial-process case demonstrating a two-tool workflow:
generate candidates with the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction
Matrix]], then **verify them with [[concept-su-field-analysis|Su-Field
analysis]]** to reduce matrix-output error (automotive parts manufacturing, 2015).

## Problem

Semi-processed automotive parts mis-align while carried through a furnace on a
long mesh-metal conveyor belt.

## TRIZ framing & tools (documented)

- The **matrix → su-field verification** sequence is the paper's methodological
  contribution: the matrix proposes solutions; su-field analysis independently
  checks them.
- Exact principle numbers and the implemented fix are in the paywalled full text
  — **unverified here**.

## Solution & outcome

Methodology-focused: demonstrates and verifies candidate solutions. No deployed
financial outcome reported.
