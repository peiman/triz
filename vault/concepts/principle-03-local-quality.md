## Overview

Inventive Principle #3 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: make different parts (or regions) non-uniform so each is suited to
its role.

## Sub-principles

- a) Change an object's structure (or its environment/influence) from uniform to
  non-uniform.
- b) Make each part of an object function in the conditions most suitable for its
  operation.
- c) Make each part fulfill a different and useful function.

## Examples

- Use a temperature, density, or pressure gradient instead of a constant one (a).
- Pencil with eraser; hammer with nail-puller (c).
