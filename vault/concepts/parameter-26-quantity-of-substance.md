## Overview

Engineering Parameter #26 of the [[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]] — the number or amount of a system materials or subsystems that might be changed. The 39 parameters form both axes of the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]]; a real problem is translated into them to look up [[concept-40-inventive-principles|inventive principles]].
