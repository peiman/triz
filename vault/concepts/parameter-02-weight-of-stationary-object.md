## Overview

Engineering Parameter #2 of the [[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]] — the mass of a non-moving object, and the force it exerts on its support or suspension. The 39 parameters form both axes of the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]]; a real problem is translated into them to look up [[concept-40-inventive-principles|inventive principles]].

## Connections

The moving/stationary counterpart of [[parameter-01-weight-of-moving-object|Weight of Moving Object]] within the 39 parameters.
