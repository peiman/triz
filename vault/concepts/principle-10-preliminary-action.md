## Overview

Inventive Principle #10 (triz.org variant: "Prior Action"): perform a required
change, or pre-position objects, before they are needed.

## Sub-principles

- a) Perform, before it is needed, the required change of an object (fully or
  partially).
- b) Pre-arrange objects so they can act from the most convenient place without
  losing time for delivery.

## Examples

- Pre-pasted wallpaper (a).
- Kanban arrangements in a Just-In-Time factory (b).
