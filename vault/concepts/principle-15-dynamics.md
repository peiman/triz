## Overview

Inventive Principle #15 (triz.org variant: "Dynamicity"): let characteristics
change to stay optimal; make rigid things movable/adaptive. Closely tied to the
[[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|trend of increasing dynamization]].

## Sub-principles

- a) Allow (or design) characteristics of an object/environment/process to change
  to find an optimal operating condition.
- b) Divide an object into parts capable of moving relative to each other.
- c) If an object or process is rigid or inflexible, make it movable or adaptive.

## Examples

- Adjustable steering wheel, seat, or mirror (a).
- A flexible boroscope for examining engines (c).

## Connections

Co-occurs with [[principle-07-nested-doll|Nested Doll]]; expresses the
dynamization trend of system evolution.
