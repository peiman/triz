## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "as an
engineering system evolves, characteristics of its components become more
coordinated with each other and with the supersystem."

## Key Properties

- Four internal mechanisms (coordination of): **shape**, **rhythms** (incl.
  resonance), **materials**, and **actions** (point 0D → line → surface → volume,
  or the reverse).
- Sub-trends: [[trend-increasing-controllability|increasing controllability]] and
  [[trend-uneven-development|uneven development of system components]].
- Maps to Altshuller's **Law of harmonization (coordination) of rhythms** (direct
  descendant — inference).

## Connections

Parent of the controllability and uneven-development sub-trends.
