## Overview

Engineering Parameter #1 of the [[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]] — the mass of a movable object, and the force it exerts on its support or suspension while in motion. The 39 parameters form both axes of the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]]; a real problem is translated into them to look up [[concept-40-inventive-principles|inventive principles]].

## Connections

The moving/stationary counterpart of [[parameter-02-weight-of-stationary-object|Weight of Stationary Object]] within the 39 parameters.
