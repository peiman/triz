## Overview

Inventive Principle #18 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: oscillate or vibrate an object, up to resonance and beyond.

## Sub-principles

- a) Cause an object to oscillate or vibrate.
- b) Increase its frequency (even to ultrasonic).
- c) Use the object's resonant frequency.
- d) Use piezoelectric vibrators instead of mechanical ones.
- e) Use combined ultrasonic and electromagnetic field oscillations.

## Examples

- An electric carving knife with vibrating blades (a).
- Break up kidney stones with ultrasonic resonance (c); quartz-crystal
  oscillation drives accurate clocks (d).
