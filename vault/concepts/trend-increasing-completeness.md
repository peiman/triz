## Overview

A [[concept-laws-of-engineering-system-evolution|TESE trend]] (MATRIZ): "as an
engineering system evolves, it acquires the following typical function blocks:
operating agent, transmission, energy source, and control system."

## Key Properties

- Four functional blocks: **operating agent** (performs the main function),
  **energy source**, **transmission** (source → operating agent), **control block**.
- Systems typically begin with only an operating agent and acquire the others as
  they mature.
- Maps to Altshuller's **Law of completeness of the parts of the system** (direct
  descendant — inference).
- Has a sub-trend: [[trend-decreasing-human-involvement|decreasing human
  involvement]].

## Connections

The MATRIZ form of the completeness law.
