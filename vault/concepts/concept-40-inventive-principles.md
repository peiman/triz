## Overview

The **40 Inventive Principles** are generalized, domain-independent heuristics
for resolving [[concept-technical-contradiction|technical contradictions]],
abstracted by Altshuller from patent analysis. Each cell of the
[[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]] points to the principle
numbers most frequently used to resolve a given improving-vs-worsening parameter
pair; the solver then re-specializes the abstract principle to the concrete
problem. They can also be used independently as a brainstorming checklist.

The set grew over time: ~5 principles by 1956, 18 by 1959, 31 by 1964, and the
full **40 by 1971**.

## The 40 Principles

Standard English naming (per the de-facto-standard triz40.com list); common
variants in parentheses. Numbering 1–40 is stable; exact English phrasing is
translation-dependent.

1. Segmentation
2. Taking out (Extraction / Separation)
3. Local quality
4. Asymmetry
5. Merging (Consolidation)
6. Universality
7. Nested doll ("Matryoshka" / Nesting)
8. Anti-weight (Counterweight)
9. Preliminary anti-action (Prior counteraction)
10. Preliminary action (Prior action)
11. Beforehand cushioning (Cushion in advance)
12. Equipotentiality
13. The other way round (Inversion / Do it in reverse)
14. Spheroidality – Curvature
15. Dynamics (Dynamicity)
16. Partial or excessive actions
17. Another dimension
18. Mechanical vibration
19. Periodic action
20. Continuity of useful action
21. Skipping (Rushing through)
22. "Blessing in disguise" (Convert harm into benefit)
23. Feedback
24. Intermediary (Mediator)
25. Self-service
26. Copying
27. Cheap short-living objects (Cheap disposables)
28. Mechanics substitution
29. Pneumatics and hydraulics
30. Flexible shells and thin films
31. Porous materials
32. Color changes
33. Homogeneity
34. Discarding and recovering
35. Parameter changes (Transformation of properties)
36. Phase transitions
37. Thermal expansion
38. Strong oxidants (Accelerated oxidation)
39. Inert atmosphere (Inert environment)
40. Composite materials
