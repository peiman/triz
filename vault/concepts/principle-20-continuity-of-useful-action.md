## Overview

Inventive Principle #20 of the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]]: keep all parts working usefully at full load, with no idle time.

## Sub-principles

- a) Carry on work continuously; make all parts of an object work at full load
  all the time.
- b) Eliminate all idle or intermittent actions.

## Examples

- A flywheel stores energy when a vehicle stops so the motor keeps running at
  optimum power (a).
- Print during the return stroke of a printer carriage (b).

## Connections

Contrast with [[principle-19-periodic-action|Periodic Action]].
