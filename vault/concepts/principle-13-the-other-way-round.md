## Overview

Inventive Principle #13 (triz.org variant: "Do It in Reverse"): invert the
action, fixity, or orientation.

## Sub-principles

- a) Invert the action used to solve the problem (e.g. heat instead of cool).
- b) Make movable parts (or environment) fixed, and fixed parts movable.
- c) Turn the object or process upside down.

## Examples

- To loosen stuck parts, cool the inner part instead of heating the outer (a).
- A moving sidewalk with standing people; a treadmill for walking in place (b).
