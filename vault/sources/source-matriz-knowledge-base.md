## MATRIZ TRIZ Knowledge Base

The online knowledge base of **MATRIZ** (the International TRIZ Association — the
body founded in Altshuller's tradition that certifies TRIZ practitioners). The
most authoritative *living* reference for canonical TRIZ definitions: the
[[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]] structure, the
[[concept-39-engineering-parameters|39 Engineering Parameters]] (full list
verified verbatim here), contradiction types, and
[[concept-ideality|ideality / IFR]].

Note: a separate breakaway body, "MATRIZ Official" (International TRIZ Official
Association asbl, Brussels — matriz-official.net), is a **distinct organization**
— avoid conflating the two when citing.
