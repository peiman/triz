## Spreafico & Russo — TRIZ Industrial Case Studies: A Critical Survey (2016)

A critical survey of 200+ TRIZ case papers, documenting that the literature
splits into **impact-only corporate anecdotes** (big ROI/patent numbers, no
reproducible method) versus **method-complete academic papers** (full
contradiction→principle steps, but small problems and rarely any ROI). Anchors
[[concept-triz-industrial-adoption|the industrial-adoption note]].

**Citation:** Spreafico, C. & Russo, D. (2016). TRIZ Industrial Case Studies: A
Critical Survey. *Procedia CIRP*, 39, 51–56. DOI: 10.1016/j.procir.2016.01.165
