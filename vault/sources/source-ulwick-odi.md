## Ulwick — Outcome-Driven Innovation (ODI)

Anthony Ulwick's operationalization of JTBD: jobs decomposed into measurable
**desired outcomes**, with over-served/under-served outcomes revealing where to
innovate. The outcome structure maps onto TRIZ parameters and contradictions
(improving an under-served outcome while not worsening an over-served one).

**Citation:** Ulwick, A. W. (2005). *What Customers Want.* McGraw-Hill; and
*Jobs to Be Done: Theory to Practice* (2016), Idea Bite Press. Strategyn / ODI
methodology.
