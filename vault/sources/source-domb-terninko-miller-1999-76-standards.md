## Domb, Terninko & Miller — The Seventy-Six Standard Solutions (1999)

The most widely cited English-language presentation of the
[[concept-76-standard-solutions|76 Standard Solutions]], including the mapping
between the standards and the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive
Principles]], and the multi-part "with examples" series covering all five
classes. Authoritative English secondary source; the standards themselves were
compiled by Altshuller and associates (1975–1985).

**Citation:** Domb, E., Terninko, J., & Miller, J. (1999). The Seventy-Six
Standard Solutions: How They Relate to the 40 Principles of Inventive Problem
Solving. *The TRIZ Journal*, May 1999.
