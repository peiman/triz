## Ang, Ng & Cher — Verifying Matrix Solutions Using Su-Field Analysis (2015)

A peer-reviewed paper applying the [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction
Matrix]] and then [[concept-su-field-analysis|Su-Field analysis]] as an
independent verification step on a real automotive-parts manufacturing problem.
Backs [[case-study-furnace-conveyor|the furnace-conveyor case]]. Full body
paywalled — exact principle numbers unverified here.

**Citation:** Ang, M. C., Ng, K. W., & Cher, D. T. (2015). Verifying Innovative
Solutions of TRIZ Engineering Contradiction Matrix Using Substance-Field Analysis
in Manufacturing. *Applied Mechanics and Materials*, 761, 196–201. DOI:
10.4028/www.scientific.net/AMM.761.196
