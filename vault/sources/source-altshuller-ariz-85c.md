## Altshuller — ARIZ-85C

The canonical text of **ARIZ-85C**, the last version of the Algorithm of
Inventive Problem Solving published under Altshuller's name and the version
MATRIZ treats as official. Republished by MATRIZ (The International TRIZ
Association) within *Tools of Classical TRIZ* (Philatov, Zusman, Altshuller,
Zlotin; Ideation International, 1999). The 9-part structure and step titles in
[[concept-ariz|ARIZ]] are taken verbatim from this document.

**Citation:** Philatov, V., Zusman, A., Altshuller, G., & Zlotin, B. (1999).
*Tools of Classical TRIZ* (ARIZ-85C text). Southfield, MI: Ideation
International. ISBN 978-1928747024. MATRIZ reprint:
https://matriz.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Altshuller_ARIZ-85-C_en.pdf
