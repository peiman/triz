## Terninko, Zusman & Zlotin — Systematic Innovation: An Introduction to TRIZ (1998)

A foundational English text on the modern, function-and-analysis-oriented TRIZ
toolkit (the Ideation International lineage). Notable for organizing TRIZ as an
analytical-then-solution workflow: problem formulation and causal analysis precede
the solution tools. Grounds the vault's notes on function analysis, causal
analysis, and harmful-function treatment.

**Citation:** Terninko, J., Zusman, A., & Zlotin, B. (1998). *Systematic
Innovation: An Introduction to TRIZ.* Boca Raton, FL: St. Lucie Press / CRC Press.
ISBN 1574441116.
