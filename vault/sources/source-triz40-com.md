## triz40.com

A widely used online reference hosting the de-facto-standard English names of the
[[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive Principles]] (with sub-principles
and examples) and an interactive [[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction
Matrix]]. Useful living cross-reference; per-principle prose is a modern
rendering, so citation-grade examples are taken from
[[source-domb-tate-1997-40-principles|Tate & Domb 1997]].
