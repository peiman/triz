## Mann — Hands-On Systematic Innovation for Business and Management (2007)

Darrell Mann's extension of TRIZ to **non-physical (business, management, software,
service)** domains, including a business/management contradiction set and parameters
distinct from the classical 39. Relevant to the project's software adaptation and to
function/job framing for non-engineering problems.

**Citation:** Mann, D. L. (2007). *Hands-On Systematic Innovation for Business and
Management.* IFR Press.
