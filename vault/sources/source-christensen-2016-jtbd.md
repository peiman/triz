## Christensen et al. — Jobs to Be Done (2016)

The canonical statement of the **Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD)** framework: customers
"hire" products to make progress on a *job*. A demand-side discovery lens for
finding the *right* problem — complementary to TRIZ's supply-side invention tools.

**Citation:** Christensen, C. M., Hall, T., Dillon, K., & Duncan, D. S. (2016).
*Competing Against Luck: The Story of Innovation and Customer Choice.* Harper
Business. See also "Know Your Customers' Jobs to Be Done," *Harvard Business
Review*, Sept 2016.
