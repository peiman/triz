## Altshuller — And Suddenly the Inventor Appeared (1994)

An accessible, problem-driven introduction to TRIZ, teaching the method through
worked inventive puzzles. Published under Altshuller's science-fiction pen name
**H. Altov**. Widely used as a first TRIZ text.

Original Russian: *И тут появился изобретатель* (Detskaya Literatura, Moscow,
first edition 1984; revised editions 1987, 1989, 2000). English: Technical
Innovation Center (Worcester, MA), translated by **Lev Shulyak**. First English
edition year is **flagged** — most records say 1994 (with a 1996 second edition),
though some sources claim a 1990 first translation (unverified, conflicting).

**Citation:** Altshuller, G. (Altov, H.) (1994). *And Suddenly the Inventor
Appeared: TRIZ, the Theory of Inventive Problem Solving* (L. Shulyak, Trans.).
Worcester, MA: Technical Innovation Center. (Original work published 1984.)
ISBN 096407401X.
