## Altshuller — The Innovation Algorithm (1999)

A comprehensive English presentation of TRIZ, including the
[[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive Principles]] and the method's
problem-solving apparatus. Based on Altshuller's Russian *Алгоритм изобретения*
("Algorithm of Inventing").

Original Russian: *Алгоритм изобретения* (first edition 1969; second edition
1973, Moskovsky Rabochy, Moscow). English: Technical Innovation Center
(Worcester, MA), 1999, translated/edited/annotated by **Lev Shulyak and Steven
Rodman**.

**Citation:** Altshuller, G. S. (1999). *The Innovation Algorithm: TRIZ,
Systematic Innovation and Technical Creativity* (L. Shulyak & S. Rodman, Trans.
& Eds.). Worcester, MA: Technical Innovation Center. (Original work *Алгоритм
изобретения* published 1969/1973.) ISBN 0964074044 · Open Library OL8525208M.
