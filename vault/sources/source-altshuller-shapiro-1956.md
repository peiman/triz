## Altshuller & Shapiro — On the Psychology of Inventive Creation (1956)

The founding paper of TRIZ. Altshuller and Rafael Shapiro argued that inventive
problem solving is not a matter of psychology or luck but follows discoverable,
teachable regularities — chiefly that hard problems contain a
[[concept-technical-contradiction|contradiction]] that must be resolved rather
than compromised. This paper seeds the entire method later elaborated in
Altshuller's books.

Published in *Voprosy Psikhologii* (Вопросы психологии — "Questions of
Psychology"), No. 6, 1956, pp. 37–49. By 1956 the method recognized only ~5 of
what would become the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive Principles]].

An English translation circulates via the WUMM Project (linked above) and the
Internet Archive; the translator of those specific copies is **unverified**.

**Citation:** Altshuller, G. S. & Shapiro, R. B. (1956). О психологии
изобретательского творчества [On the psychology of inventive creation].
*Voprosy Psikhologii*, (6), 37–49.
