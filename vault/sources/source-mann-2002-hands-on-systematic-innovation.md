## Darrell Mann — Hands-On Systematic Innovation (2002)

A widely used modern TRIZ reference. Notable here for its compatibility list
organizing the [[concept-40-inventive-principles|40 Inventive Principles]] under
the four [[concept-separation-principles|Separation Principles]], and (with
colleagues) for the modernized **"Matrix 2003"** rebuilt from contemporary
patents to address the dated-data critique of Altshuller's original
[[concept-contradiction-matrix|Contradiction Matrix]].

**Citation:** Mann, D. L. (2002). *Hands-On Systematic Innovation.* Ieper,
Belgium: CREAX Press.
