## Beckmann — TRIZ Principles for Information Technology (2015)

A peer-reviewed analysis finding that post-2000 attempts to map the 40 principles
1:1 onto software "had limited success," and instead decomposing IT into
objects/data/algorithms to derive **IT-native principles deliberately different from
the original 40**. Independent corroboration that the classical matrix/principles do
not transfer cleanly to software.

**Citation:** Beckmann, H. (2015). TRIZ Principles for Information Technology.
*Procedia Engineering*, 131, 993–1001. (Detail partly paywalled — existence and
core claim verified; internal specifics flagged medium confidence.)
