## Altshuller — Creativity as an Exact Science (1984)

A foundational TRIZ monograph presenting the theory systematically: the role of
[[concept-technical-contradiction|contradictions]], the standards for inventive
solutions, and the trend of systems toward [[concept-ideality|ideality]].

Original Russian: *Творчество как точная наука: теория решения изобретательских
задач* (Sovetskoe Radio, Moscow, 1979, "Cybernetics" series). English edition
published 1984 by Gordon and Breach (New York), "Studies in Cybernetics" v. 5;
later reissued by Routledge / Taylor & Francis. Translator reported as **Anthony
Williams** (single-source — treat as moderately confirmed).

**Citation:** Altshuller, G. S. (1984). *Creativity as an Exact Science: The
Theory of the Solution of Inventive Problems* (A. Williams, Trans.). New York:
Gordon and Breach. (Original work published 1979, Sovetskoe Radio, Moscow.)
ISBN 0677212305 · OCLC 9918708.
