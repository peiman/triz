## Nakagawa — USIT (Unified Structured Inventive Thinking) / Software TRIZ

Toru Nakagawa's USIT is a streamlined, function-centric reformulation of TRIZ that
is well-suited to software because it foregrounds **functions and attributes**
(which are what software systems are made of) rather than physical parameters. His
TRIZ/USIT home page is a large independent archive of method papers and software
applications.

**Citation:** Nakagawa, T. (2006 and ongoing). *USIT (Unified Structured Inventive
Thinking) and Software TRIZ.* TRIZ Home Page in Japan, Osaka Gakuin University.
Retrieved 2026-05-31.
