## Souchkov — xTRIZ and Root-Conflict Analysis (RCA+)

Valeri Souchkov's body of modern-TRIZ work (xTRIZ methodology, ICG Training &
Consulting). Notable for **Root-Conflict Analysis (RCA+)** — a causal-analysis
method that derives contradictions from a chain of disadvantages and resolves them
via separation, **sidestepping the contradiction matrix** entirely. Souchkov also
extended causal/contradiction analysis to **non-technical and business** domains,
which is directly relevant to software and product problems.

Key documents (independent sources): "Root Conflict Analysis (RCA+): Structured
Problems and Contradictions Mapping" (2011), and "Application of Root-Conflict
Analysis (RCA+) to Formulate Inventive Problems in Non-Technical Areas" (ETRIA TRIZ
Future Conference, 2007).

**Citation:** Souchkov, V. *xTRIZ Methodology and Root-Conflict Analysis (RCA+).*
